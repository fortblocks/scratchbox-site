Privacy

Scratchbox never uploads your files. Capture never uploads your captures. There is no account.

Who we are

Scratchbox

Scratchbox is a macOS menu-bar inbox for screenshots and throwaway files, plus Scratchbox Capture, a Chrome extension. The public site is scratchbox.xyz. Contact: chris@ridiculouscorp.com.

Mac app

Nothing leaves the machine.

The Mac app creates folders on your disk (~/Scratchbox/Inbox, Kept, Expired) and, if you turn it on, points macOS screenshots at Inbox. Watch Downloads only moves new finished files from ~/Downloads into Inbox. Files stay on your Mac. The app does not create an account, does not phone home, and does not upload file contents.

Chrome extension

Capture is local too.

Scratchbox Capture takes a screenshot or recording when you ask, then saves a file into Chrome’s Downloads folder using the browser’s download API. It does not send page contents, screenshots, recordings, URLs, or microphone audio to Scratchbox servers — there are none for this product.

Permissions exist so Capture can do that job on the current tab:

  • See site contents / all URLs — required to screenshot or record the page you chose, including entire-page stitch. Not used to scrape the web in the background.
  • Capture tab / screen — only when you click Record.
  • Microphone — only if you tick Microphone, and only for that recording.
  • Downloads — to write Screenshot …png or Screen Recording …webm into Downloads.
  • Storage — remembers whether tab audio and microphone are checked, on this browser only.

The extension does not sell data, does not use it for advertising, does not run analytics, and does not share captures with third parties. Chrome itself may show its own permission and download prompts.
